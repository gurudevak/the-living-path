Module Two

A Deep Work with the Mind

2-8 November 2026 · North End Farm, Dorset

Most of us have been told, one way or another, that the mind is the problem. Quiet it. Get past it. Rise above it.

That works for a while. It also leaves you at odds with the part of you that runs your entire day, your life eventually.

We’re going another way this week.

The depth you’re looking for sits underneath the mind. Befriending your mind is the route to take. And befriending your mind means opening your heart to yourself — all of yourself, including what you’d rather hadn’t turned up.

So a deep work with the mind is at the same time a deep work with the heart.

The two don’t come apart, and the whole week is built on that.

This is Raja Yoga. Royal yoga. It develops the consciousness to serve all, and it starts with the all of you.

Meeting the Whole of Your Consciousness

We spend real time getting to know its different parts, because they want different things.

There’s the conscious mind, running your day through your personality. It holds this life’s memories and this life’s programming. It’s curious and it wants to be useful — which is exactly why it picked up so much from your family, your schooling, your religion, and everyone around you, without anyone asking your permission.

There’s the subconscious, sitting underneath your choices and doing a lot of the driving. Your hopes are in there. So is material carried from other lifetimes and other parts of your soul. It works while you sleep. When a thought arrives from nowhere, that’s where it came from.

There’s the ego, the personality mind, running on emotion and wanting and needing.

And there’s the higher mind, your link to the Creator Self. It isn’t in a hurry. It isn’t attached to time and it isn’t chasing an outcome. It sees the whole thing and it knows when to wait. It sits at a higher frequency, so you don’t bring it down to you. You rise to meet it.

Then there’s the feminine mind — wisdom, love, connectedness — and the masculine mind — knowledge, light, action.

Meditation is how the balancing happens. It brings calm and clarity, and it teaches you to tell the difference between what’s happening and what your mind has laid on top of what’s happening. That’s a bigger skill than it sounds. It is a path in itself to come into the fullness of our being.

The Royal Path of Meditation

The Yoga Sutras of Patanjali — the Royal Path of Meditation — define yoga as the stilling of the fluctuations of the mind.

We work through Patanjali’s eight limbs — yama (ethics), niyama (observances), asana (posture), pranayama (breath), pratyahara (senses inward), dharana (concentration), dhyana (sustained attention), samadhi (absorption). The last four are the inner ones, the ones that work on the mind directly.

We’ll use mindfulness, Kundalini Yoga kriyas, concentration practices, inquiry, and open awareness and specific meditations to balance, calm, open and direct our minds as portals into higher consciousness.

The Day on the Heart

One whole day goes to the heart, because it’s the way in — to consciousness, and to the higher mind.

Patanjali says so directly. Sutra III.34: concentration on the heart brings knowledge of consciousness. And in the chakra map the heart is the middle of things, three centres below it and three above, where the everyday and the vast meet.

That day is about self-love. About holding all of yourself, including the parts you keep out of view.

It’s also where the befriending becomes possible, because you can’t befriend anything you’re still judging. We’ll use meditations that wake up a compassionate heart, and we’ll take the steps needed to drop into that place and hold everything you carry, in coherency, in oneness.

Time for profound Contemplation

Where does your attention go when you stop steering it? Which of your beliefs are actually yours? What do you do when something can’t be fixed — and what do you do when something can’t be got back? What have you been waiting to improve about yourself before you’d let yourself be at peace with it? And what is your mind adding, right now, to what’s really here?

Between Glastonbury and the Jurassic Coast

North End Farm, Dorset

Location matters. When you're liberating the elements within yourself, the land you're on becomes part of the work.

North End Farm

A thatched farmhouse dating to before 1600, with parts rebuilt after a fire in 1640. Lovingly restored and maintained - charm and character combined with modern amenities. Stone walls, Tudor fireplace, old bread oven.

Set in secluded Dorset countryside with beautiful outdoor space for walking and sitting.

The Landscape

You're in ancient sacred territory. Within an hour: Stanton Drew Stone Circles (3000-2000 BCE), third largest in England. Priddy Circles on the Mendips, four Bronze Age henges at the highest point of the plateau. Burial mounds, standing stones, hillforts throughout. This land has been recognized as sacred for five thousand years.

Two miles away, the Jurassic Coast - England's only natural UNESCO World Heritage Site. 185 million years of Earth's history visible in the cliffs. Walk here and you're literally walking through deep time. Red sandstone, chalk downs, clay beds full of fossils. You can watch water and wind actively reshaping ancient rock.

Forty-five minutes: Glastonbury. Earth's heart chakra. The Tor rising from flat Somerset Levels. Chalice Well with its red spring. The Michael and Mary ley lines intersecting. Abbey ruins where tradition says the first Christian church in Europe was built. People have been drawn here for millennia because something about this landscape opens the heart.

Elements Alive in the Land

Insights come. From sitting by a stone circle at dusk. Walking the coastal path in mist. Standing in a field, allowing yourself to simply be present.

We'll take time for this. Space for solo walking, sitting outside, learning how to be with the land in your own way to receive what is needed for you in that moment. Group practices outdoors when weather permits. The landscape becomes part of our circle, part of what teaches and holds us.

When you're working to discover how the elements move uniquely through you, being in a place where their power has been honored for millennia matters. The land holds what you're ready to find.

Frequently Asked Questions

What you might be wondering

Do I need to be a yoga teacher to apply?+
No. While this training draws from yogic traditions (particularly Kundalini Yoga) and energy healing work, it's designed for anyone who holds space for transformation - teachers, healers, coaches, therapists, bodyworkers, facilitators of any kind. The elemental framework and practices are universal and can be integrated into whatever work you do.
What if I'm not familiar with Kundalini Yoga?+
That's perfectly fine. While we'll use some Kundalini Yoga practices and concepts, you don't need prior experience with this tradition. The focus is on working with universal principles (the elements, energy, embodiment) that exist across all spiritual and healing traditions. If you have a committed practice of some kind, you'll be able to engage with this work.
What if I can't attend all five modules?+
The training is designed as a complete path, with each module building on the last, but you don't have to commit to all five at once. You can take a module and decide about continuing afterward. If you're joining now you would start with Module Two - Module One ran in June 2026, and runs again only when the cycle starts over, after all five have been completed.
What's the daily schedule like?+
Days begin with morning practice around 6:00 AM, followed by breakfast and teaching sessions throughout the day. Afternoons typically include practice, study, and time for integration. Evenings offer group discussions and closing practices. We'll also take one or two excursions to local sacred sites during the week, which will follow a different rhythm. The schedule balances structure with flexibility, intensity with rest.
What should I bring?+
A yoga mat, meditation cushion or bench, comfortable clothing for practice, layers for varying temperatures, journal and pens, any props you prefer for your practice, and an open heart. Detailed packing information will be sent upon registration.
How does this differ from other advanced trainings?+
This isn't a certification program in the traditional sense. It's an immersion training focused on deepening your own practice and your capacity to serve from that depth. While it will enhance your facilitation skills, the primary focus is inner work and embodiment rather than curriculum and technique.
Will I receive a certificate?+
You'll receive a certificate of attendance after each module you complete.
What if I have physical limitations or health concerns?+
Please contact us directly to discuss. The practices can often be modified to work with different body needs, but it's important we talk about your specific situation before you register.
Can I arrive early or stay late?+
The farmhouse may be available for an extra night before or after the training week. Please inquire about availability when registering.
What is your cancellation policy?+

Module One: Your Elemental Magic

The Gift the Five Elements Weave in You

This module ran 15-21 June 2026. It runs again when the cycle starts over, after all five modules have been completed - so if you are joining now, you would start with Module Two.

We begin where all creation begins: with the elements. Earth, water, fire, air, and ether are the fundamental forces that weave through your body, your consciousness, your work, and the natural world around you.

Something in you already knows there is more available. You've given so much to your path — your practice, your people, the work you feel called to do. And through all of that giving, a quieter knowing has been growing: that there is a deeper source waiting to meet you. A place where your energy renews rather than depletes. Where the passion that first drew you to this work isn't just a memory — it's alive, present, and ready to move through you again.

This module is about coming home to that aliveness. When you reconnect with the elemental forces that are alive in you — not as concepts but as felt, embodied experience — something shifts. Your spirit remembers what it came here to do. The heaviness lifts. A natural vitality returns, not because you've pushed harder, but because you've finally made room for what was always there.

Liberating the Elements Within You

This is what this first week is really about.

The elements have been defined, categorized, taught in specific ways. You've learned what earth is supposed to feel like. What fire is meant to do. How water should flow. As if there's only one way to experience them.

The elements alive in you are waiting to be discovered, not confirmed. Your earth might ground in ways traditional teachings never described. Your fire might burn with a quality that doesn't fit the usual definitions. Your water might move in patterns uniquely yours.

When you liberate the elements within yourself, you free them from how they're supposed to be.

These times are calling for new expressions, fresh perspectives, ways of working that haven't been mapped yet. Building and expanding on the old. The elements want to move through you in ways that serve what's emerging. Your unique configuration is not random. It's what's needed.

Through this deep understanding, you'll bring your way of working from deep within you. You'll discover how to allow new insights to arise naturally from your practice. You'll learn how to contribute to life in ways you may have never imagined possible, but you realise are very natural to you.

Through specific practices drawn from yogic and energetic traditions, you'll develop a feeling that you sense of how earth grounds, how water flows, how fire transforms, how air moves, and how ether holds space for it all.

The Power of Kriya

The power of kriya is at the heart of this module. In yogic tradition, kriya means "complete action" or "sequence" - it's the technology of creation itself. This is Kundalini Yoga's core gift - kriya as living technology. Understanding how to work with it at this depth equips you for what these times are calling forth. This will transform how you attract teaching kriyas going forward.

When you understand kriya as elemental flow, as the alive process within you and within all life, your work shifts. You're facilitating a living process by honouring it. You're working with the same forces that create mountains, move oceans, and birth stars.
